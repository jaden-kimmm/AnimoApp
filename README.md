# Animo
### Every stride, understood.

**Animo** is an emotion-based running companion app designed to align your workout with your feelings. Whether you're stressed, happy, or tired, Animo adapts its interface and feedback to support you.

## Features

- **Emotion-Adaptive UI**: The app's colors, gradients, and animations shift dynamically based on your selected emotion (e.g., Calm Blue for Stressed, Energetic Yellow for Happy).
- **Personalized Onboarding**: A tailored intake flow to gauge your fitness level, goals, and coaching preferences.
- **Run Tracking**: Minimalist tracking interface for Distance, Time, Pace, and Calories.
- **Visual Route Gradients**: Routes are visualized with emotion-themed gradients.
- **Post-Run Insights**: Summary screens that reflect on your performance and emotional state.

## Tech Stack

- **Language**: Swift 6
- **Framework**: SwiftUI
- **Design System**: Custom typography and color palettes (`AppColor`, `TextStyle`).
- **Assets**: Custom vector assets for route shapes and intensity indicators.

## Requirements

- iOS 17.0+
- Xcode 15.0+
